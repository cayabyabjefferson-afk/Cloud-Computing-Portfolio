2. What similarities exist among AWS, Azure, and GCP?
   
- AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP) have many similarities. All three provide virtual machines, storage, networking, databases, identity and access management, and security services. They also provide global infrastructure that allows organizations to deploy applications in different locations. Another similarity is that all three platforms support scalability, allowing organizations to increase or decrease resources depending on their workload.

3. How do business requirements influence cloud platform selection?
   
- Business requirements have an important influence on cloud platform selection. A company should consider its budget, existing technology, security requirements, workload, performance needs, scalability, and future growth. For example, Azure can be a good choice for an organization that already uses Microsoft technologies, while Google Cloud can be attractive for AI and machine learning workloads. Therefore, the best cloud provider depends on the specific needs of the organization rather than simply choosing the most popular provider.
